Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 2026

chatmany is self-hosted — each instance is run independently by the Instagram account it's connected to. There's no central chatmany company holding your data; whatever this instance has stored about you lives only in its owner's own database.

If you commented or messaged an account running chatmany

To have your data removed:

  1. Message the connected Instagram account directly and ask the owner to delete your data, or
  2. Use Instagram's own Settings → Apps and Websites → Tester Invites / Active Apps to revoke chatmany's access to your account, if you were ever added as a tester or connected your own account to the app.

The owner can remove a person's record — their conversation state, comment text, follow status, and email if captured — from the database on request. This deletes:

  • Their conversation/funnel state for that campaign
  • Any captured email address
  • Their entries in the comment-processing ledger

Aggregate, de-identified event counts used for the dashboard (e.g. "12 comments this month") may remain, since they no longer reference a specific person once the underlying record is removed.

If you're the account owner

Disconnecting Instagram from the Automations app immediately stops all further processing. To remove a specific person's data, delete their row from the conversations table (and processed_comments if you want it fully forgotten) in your instance's D1 database.

Contact

For a specific chatmany instance, contact goes to whichever Instagram account it's connected to — that's the data owner and controller for this deployment.
