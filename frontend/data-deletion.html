Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 2026

FadeLoop is self-hosted — each instance is run independently by the Instagram account it's connected to. There's no central FadeLoop company holding your data; whatever this instance has stored about you lives only in its owner's own database.

If you commented or messaged an account running FadeLoop

To have your data removed:

  1. Message the connected Instagram account directly and ask the owner to delete your data, or
  2. Use Instagram's own Settings → Apps and Websites → Tester Invites / Active Apps to revoke FadeLoop's access to your account, if you were ever added as a tester or connected your own account to the app.

The owner can remove a person's record — their conversation state, comment text, follow status, and email if captured — from the database on request. This deletes:

  • Their conversation/funnel state for that campaign
  • Any captured email address
  • Their entries in the comment-processing ledger

Aggregate, de-identified event counts used for the dashboard (e.g. "12 comments this month") may remain, since they no longer reference a specific person once the underlying record is removed.

If you're the account owner

Open Account & appearance → Disconnect Instagram and confirm with your FadeLoop login password to permanently delete all local automations, contacts, analytics and folders. Your FadeLoop account and sessions remain available. This disconnects this installation without revoking Meta permissions; messages already submitted cannot be recalled. To remove only a specific person's data, delete their row from the conversations table (and processed_comments if you want it fully forgotten) in your instance's D1 database.

Contact

For a specific FadeLoop instance, contact goes to whichever Instagram account it's connected to — that's the data owner and controller for this deployment.
